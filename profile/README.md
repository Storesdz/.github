**From your first product to your next delivery.**

- **Your storefront** — choose a theme and make it your own.
- **Your catalogue** — manage photos, variants, prices and stock.
- **Your orders** — handle cash on delivery, confirmation calls and local shipping.
- **Your campaigns** — build product landing pages with connected checkout.

### For developers

Our public [funnel SDK](https://github.com/Storesdz/funnel-sdk) connects custom HTML landing pages to StoresDZ products, delivery options and checkout. Find the API, examples and development commands in its README.

Most platform repositories are private. Contributions and bug reports for the SDK are welcome through [issues](https://github.com/Storesdz/funnel-sdk/issues) and [pull requests](https://github.com/Storesdz/funnel-sdk/pulls).
